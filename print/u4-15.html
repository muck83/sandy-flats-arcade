Fill in the notes

1. A fee is paid only once. On the graph it is the start value, b.

2. A is paid for each use: each night, visit or km. On the graph it is the slope, m.

3. The point is where two options cost the same. On a graph, the two lines there.

4. Before the break-even, the option with the smaller fee is cheaper. After it, the option with the smaller is cheaper.

5. Find the break-even with any method: graphing, or elimination. Then answer in a with units.

Worked example: Gym A costs 100 SAR to join + 20 SAR a visit. Gym B costs 30 SAR a visit, no joining fee.
