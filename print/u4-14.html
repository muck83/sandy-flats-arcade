2. Use a boundary line for ≤ or ≥. Use a line for < or >.

3. Put a point like (0, 0) in the inequality. True: shade its side. False: shade the other side.

4. The solution of a system of inequalities is the region where the shadings .

5. In a real situation, a point is only if it is in the region AND makes sense.

6. New: a corner of the region is where two lines cross. Find it exactly by solving the system of (substitution or elimination). A corner on a dashed line is a solution.
