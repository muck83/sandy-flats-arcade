1. The graph of a function is all the points ( , ). The x-value is the and the y-value is the .

2. To test a graph, imagine a line sliding across it from left to right.

3. If the line ever touches the graph at or more points, one input has more than one . The graph is a function.

4. If every vertical line touches the graph at most , it is a function.

Why it works

Two points stacked on the same vertical line have the same x (input) but different y (outputs). That breaks the rule "one input, one output".
