Four stories, four graphs, no numbers. The vertical label is missing (?). Use the shape.

1. Lina's walk. Lina walks from home at a steady pace. She stops at a shop to buy water. Then she is late, so she runs the rest of the way to school.

2. Bath time. The tub fills with water at a steady rate. Yusuf sits in the bath for a while. Then he pulls the plug and the water drains out quickly.

3. Camel trek. A camel walks slowly away from the desert camp. It rests at an oasis. Then it walks back to camp at the same slow speed.

4. Hot tea. A cup of hot tea is poured. It cools down fast at first. Then it cools more and more slowly.
