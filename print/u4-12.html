The school play sold 40 tickets for 670 SAR. Adult tickets cost 25 SAR. Student tickets cost 10 SAR. How many of each were sold?

Planning stepWhat I write
Define variablesLet a = number of adult tickets, s = number of student tickets
Fact 1: 40 ticketsa + s = 40  (counts the tickets)
Fact 2: 670 SAR25a + 10s = 670  (counts the money)
Solve (substitution)s = 40 − a → 25a + 400 − 10a = 670 → 15a = 270 → a = 18, s = 22
Answer + check18 adult and 22 student tickets.  450 + 220 = 670 SAR ✓  18 + 22 = 40 ✓
