Mini-lesson · 5 min I can use two points on each line to decide if two lines cross, and find where.
Fill in the notes

1. Slope from two points: m = change in ÷ change in = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁)

2. To find b: put the slope and one point into y = mx + b, then solve for .

3. Different slopes: the lines cross at exactly point. The system has one solution.

4. Same slope, different b: the lines are . They never cross: solution.

5. Same slope and same b: it is the line. Infinitely many solutions.

6. To find the crossing, set the two equations and solve for x. Then find y.

Worked example: Line A goes through (0, 2) and (4, 10). Line B goes through (1, 7) and (3, 5).
