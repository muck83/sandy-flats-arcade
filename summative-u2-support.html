Words in this task

  • Constraint (limit): a rule a plan has to follow, like the floor space or the budget.
  • System: all the inequalities together. A plan has to pass every one.
  • Boundary line: the line you get when you change the inequality sign to =. Solid if the line is included (≤ or ≥), dashed if it isn't (< or >).
  • Test point: a point you put into an inequality to see which side of the line to keep. (0, 0) is the easiest, if it isn't on the line.
  • Feasible region: the area where every inequality is true: all the possible plans.
  • Corner: a point where two edges of the feasible region meet. The best plan is always at a corner.

Worked example: the Taco Truck

A different problem, worked all the way through, so you can see every step. Use the same steps on the Pop-Up Arcade.

The Taco TruckA food truck sells tacos (t) and burritos (b) at lunch. A taco takes 2 minutes on the grill and a burrito takes 4 minutes. The grill has 240 minutes. There are only 80 tortillas (one per taco or burrito). The truck promised the office next door at least 10 burritos. A taco makes $3 profit and a burrito makes $5. How many of each should they make for the most profit?
  1. One variable. "At least 10 burritos": b ≥ 10, closed dot on 10, arrow right. Tacos only: grill 2t ≤ 240, so t ≤ 120, and tortillas t ≤ 80. Both are true when t ≤ 80.
  2. Two variables. Grill: 2t + 4b ≤ 240 (2t = minutes for tacos, 4b = minutes for burritos). Tortillas: t + b ≤ 80. The system: b ≥ 10, t ≥ 0, 2t + 4b ≤ 240, t + b ≤ 80.
  3. Test a plan. 50 tacos and 40 burritos: grill 2(50) + 4(40) = 260. Is 260 ≤ 240? No: ✗, so the plan doesn't work.
  4. Slope-intercept form. 2t + 4b = 240 → 4b = −2t + 240 → b = −½t + 60. Meaning: every extra burrito uses the grill time of 2 tacos.
  5. Intercepts. Grill: t = 0 gives b = 60, so (0, 60). b = 0 gives t = 120, so (120, 0). Tortillas: (0, 80) and (80, 0). Both lines are solid (≤).
  6. Shade. Test (0, 0): 0 ≤ 240 ✓ and 0 ≤ 80 ✓, so keep the side with (0, 0) for both. Keep above b = 10.
  7. Crossing point. The lines cross at (40, 40). Check: 2(40) + 4(40) = 240 ✓ and 40 + 40 = 80 ✓.
  8. Corners and profit with P = 3t + 5b: (0, 10) → $50 · (70, 10) → $260 · (40, 40) → $320 · (0, 60) → $300. The best plan is 40 tacos and 40 burritos.
  9. Argue. "Make 40 tacos and 40 burritos for $320 profit. It uses all the grill time and all the tortillas. Burritos only (60) makes $300, which is $20 less."

Boathouse floor plan

The boathouse floor is 10 m by 6 m: 60 m². Each square is 1 m². Cut out the machines and try plans on the floor. A claw machine is 1 m × 2 m (2 m²), an air hockey table with room for the players is 2 m × 3 m (6 m²). Think about it: do the machines always fit when the area adds up to 60 m² or less? Why does the task only count area?
