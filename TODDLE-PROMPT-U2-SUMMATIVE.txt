THE ASSIGNMENT (attach to Day 5, due end of Day 5)
Title: Unit 2 Summative: The Pop-Up Arcade
Submission: file upload (photos, PDF or video). More than one file allowed, if Toddle has that setting.
Instructions:
Upload your finished Pop-Up Arcade Plan. It must have all six parts: the question and your variables, the limits table
with number lines, the system of inequalities, the graph with the feasible region, the corner table, and your
recommendation (with the what-if and your news flash).
- On paper: a clear photo of every page (flat, good light, the whole page), or one scanned PDF.
- Typed on the website: press "Print or save my work as PDF", then choose Save as PDF.
- A pitch video: upload the video, plus a photo of your graph and table.
Task: https://muck83.github.io/sandy-flats-arcade/summative-u2.html
Rubric (levels: Limited, Emerging, Developed = the goal, Deep):
- C1 Concepts · Writing and solving inequalities
  Limited: some inequalities, but the sign or terms often don't match the words.
  Emerging: most one-variable inequalities right; two-variable ones have errors.
  Developed: every one-variable inequality written and solved; every inequality in the system right (> for staff pay).
  Deep: Developed, plus explains each sign and the meaning of the slopes as trades between machines.
- C1 Concepts · Graphing
  Limited: number lines or boundary lines mostly not correct.
  Emerging: number lines mostly right; some boundary lines right, but shading or solid/dashed mixed up.
  Developed: dots right; every boundary line right and labelled, staff pay dashed, feasible region shaded correctly.
  Deep: Developed, plus every corner found and the crossing point checked in both equations.
- C3 Problem solving · Making the decision
  Limited: chooses a plan with little use of the limits.
  Emerging: tests some plans or finds the money for some points, but doesn't reach the best plan.
  Developed: tests the corners, finds the best whole-number plan, shows why each friend's plan is worse or not possible.
  Deep: Developed, plus the what-if and news flash answered correctly, with reasons.
- C2 Communicating · The finished plan
  Limited: hard to follow; parts missing, few labels.
  Emerging: most parts there; some working, labels or units missing.
  Developed: all six parts there; variables defined, labels and units, a clear recommendation with numbers.
  Deep: Developed, plus clear and convincing enough that someone new to the task could check every step.
