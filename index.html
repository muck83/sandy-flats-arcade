Sandy Flats Arcade

Games that put you inside the films. Feed the machines, bust the liars, crack the rules. Every game has a way in for everyone and a ceiling nobody hits by accident.

The games

One game for each stretch of the films. About 30 minutes each: a low floor anyone can start, a core path that earns the standard certificate, and stretch levels for the special ones.

Effie's Lost Reels

Rare clips that turn up as rewards for real effort: a funny moment from the films, a deleted scene, or a blooper. They only appear at natural pauses, at most twice in a session. Some hide a maths joke.

For teachers

Check a certificate in the Keeper's Ledger. Open teacher mode with Ctrl+Shift+K (or the button below) to switch reels, timers, hints or voices, show level-skip buttons, or run a Class Unlock.

Crack the Fair: when and how

  • When: right after Zero 2: Sidekick. Replay the stretch level "The Jammed Slot" the day after Episode B, Where Zero Isn't.
  • Hook (5 min): play Zero 2 from about 4:00. Pause on "WHAT DO I GIVE FOR FOUR?" and take predictions.
  • Set-up: pairs, one device. One feeds, one writes the tape on paper; swap each machine.
  • Timing: low floor 6 min, cousins 8, Marcus and Maximus 8, stretch 8+.
  • Certificate moment (minute 35): pairs compare inputs used. The pair with the fewest explains how they did it. Expect "zero first, then one".
  • Exit ticket: 0 → −4 and 1 → −1. Write the rule. What does it give for 10?

Gus's Forecast: when and how

  • When: right after Episode A, The Weather Line, before any equations. It uses the episode's stories: Gus's bucket, Mr. Dunmore's walk (walk, tea, run), Marcus's bike speed over the hill, the shop trip and SFN's Graph of the Week.
  • Hook (5 min): replay the moment Marcus draws his bike ride shaped like the hill. Pause and ask: is that a picture of his speed, or of the road?
  • Set-up: individuals, with mini whiteboards. Before each builder level, students sketch the graph by hand, then build it.
  • Timing: bucket 3 min, read it like a sentence 5, Dunmore 3, bike ride 5, shop 3, Graph of the Week 4, stretch 7+.
  • Certificate moment: students read their Backwards Prophet forecast aloud; the class draws it from the words alone, then compares.
  • Who goes for what: on track, The Weather Line (steady means straight: a bridge to Episode D); writers, Backwards Prophet.
  • Exit ticket: sketch your distance from home: walk to a friend's house, stay for an hour, then get a lift home.

Glitch Hunt: when and how

  • When: right after Lost in Graph Land (Linesy is in case 4). Cases 0 to 3 also work straight after The Glitch.
  • Hook (5 min): play The Glitch from about 3:30. Stop at Marcus's "Seize him!" and take a vote: is he right?
  • Set-up: individuals, then run case 2 as a whole-class bust on the board: you play Marcus, the class stops you with evidence.
  • Timing: snack machine 3 min, case 1 5, case 2 6 (whole class), cases 3 and 4 10, stretch 6+.
  • Certificate moment: students read a case report aloud: "Suspect __, input __ gave __ and __."
  • Who goes for what: students who think a repeated output breaks a function go for Friend of Sam; on track, Efficient Detective; racing ahead, Rule Breaker, Rule Maker, then swap devices so a partner hunts their liar.
  • Exit ticket: a mapping diagram with a repeated output. Function? Why?

Scorch Marks: when and how

  • When: right after Episode C, One Alone. It uses the episode's exact trail: stones (1, −1) and (5, 19), the lock at (4, 14), the stairs 5/1 = 10/2 = 20/4 and the decoy with slope −2. The Crossing stretch level is also a good review after The Undoer.
  • Hook (5 min): replay One Alone where One says "four over twenty, one fifth" and flies into the hedge. Ask: what went wrong?
  • Set-up: individuals, with squared paper. Ask each student to draw one pair of similar slope triangles by hand to staple to their certificate.
  • Timing: staircase 3 min, stones and rise over run 6, big and little stairs 6, survey marks 4, backwards and downhill 5, the lock 4, stretch 8+.
  • Certificate moment: find the biggest and smallest hand-drawn triangles in the room on the same line. Same rise ÷ run?
  • Who goes for what: proof-ready students go for Triangle Prover; ahead on equations, Same Place, Same Time; on track, Where One Starts (good preparation for Episode B's two points not at 0).
  • Exit ticket: points (2, 5) and (6, 7). Slope? Draw two different triangles to show it.

Faster Than: when and how

  • When: right after Episode E, Faster Than. It uses Rita's exact signs: taxi d = 20 + 0.5t, the scooter table, the bike graph and the Sand Sled in words.
  • Hook (5 min): replay the moment Marcus grabs the taxi "because it has the biggest number". Pause and vote: is the biggest number the speed?
  • Set-up: individuals. Run "The Race Board" as a whole-class betting round: students vote with cards, then you run the clock on the board.
  • Timing: ahead or faster 3 min, signs 6, rank 4, Proportional Lane 4, race board 6, lighthouse 3, stretch 7+.
  • Certificate moment: "Helmet check": who bet the race board right before the clock ran? They explain how they read the mixed representations.
  • Who goes for what: on track, Fact Checker; racing ahead, Faster Than Fast; quick intuitive thinkers, Helmet Keeper (the hardest special in the arcade).
  • Exit ticket: A: d = 4t. B: starts at 6 m, 3 m per second. Who is ahead at t = 4? At t = 10? Which is proportional?

Linear or Liar?: when and how

  • When: right after Episode D, Linear or Liar?. It uses the episode's case files: Steady's uneven-gap table, Sam's U, Linny's 3, 5, 9, 17, the lightning-round equations and the Square-ish table. The stretch levels return to Doors and Walls (the 1/x door and the Doubler).
  • Hook (5 min): replay Doors and Walls at the 1/x door. Stop at Marcus's "zero makes the BIGGEST DOOR EVER!" Is he right? Don't resolve it: the game does.
  • Set-up: pairs as detective partners. One runs the tools, the other keeps the case notes on paper. Swap each case.
  • Timing: lift race 4 min, cases 1 and 2 8, case 3 6, lightning round 5, stretch 7+.
  • Certificate moment: a line-up of Fool the Detector tables on the board. The class tries to bust each one with one more input.
  • Who goes for what: everyone should try Door Master (it is the emotional heart of the series); racing ahead, Rule Maker; pattern spotters, The Doubler.
  • Exit ticket: x = 0, 1, 2, 3 and y = 1, 2, 4, 8. Linear or liar? Show the evidence.

Doug's Steps: when and how

  • When: right after Episode F, Doug's Steps, as the last game before the Finale. It uses the episode's tile bridge (y = 2x + 1 on 0 to 6), Doors A (8) and B (9), One's continuous flight and the way home (y = 3x − 1 on 1 to 4), plus the water tower from Start and Step.
  • Hook (5 min): replay Start and Step where Marcus lands in the crater: "Zero minutes. Fifteen litres." Then "This machine only lives from zero to fifteen." Ask: what does "lives" mean?
  • Set-up: individuals. Run "Count It or Measure It?" whole class: students hold up a dots or a line card for each picture.
  • Timing: sort 3 min, tiles 5, doors 4, dots or line 5, the way home 4, the tower 6, stretch 6+.
  • Certificate moment: Doug's approval: play a coo for each standard certificate shown. It's silly, and it works.
  • Who goes for what: on track, Edge Walker; writers, Numbers Pretending. Honorary Pigeon is secret: don't announce it. Let a student discover it.
  • Exit ticket: concert tickets cost 50 riyals each, maximum 6. Dots or a line? Domain? Range?

The Keeper's Vault: when and how

  • When: right after The Chain. "The Backwards Door" fits best after The Undoer. "The Final Chain" level is the unit review after the Finale: run it on the projector.
  • Hook (5 min): The Chain at the Keeper's Vault: Marcus's "FIRST TRY!" then "OUTRAGEOUS! IT SAID I WAS RIGHT!" Ask: how can a rule be right once and still be wrong?
  • Set-up: individuals. Optionally run Vault Run 1 as class teams on the board, four students as the four doors, against the 90-second clock (switch the timer off in teacher mode for anyone who needs it).
  • Timing: name door 3 min, run 1 7, run 2 7, notation door 4, stretch 8+.
  • Certificate moment: a "pit report": each table names the mistake that sent Marcus into the pit most often.
  • Who goes for what: on track, Link Inspector; racing ahead, Fifth Door Architect (then swap doors with a partner); strong on undoing, The Undoer's Apprentice.
  • Final Chain lesson: whole class, projector mode, one link at a time. Then the Class Unlock with the word RAIN for the blooper reel.

Crack the Fair: who goes for which certificate

  • Needs support: stay in the cousins with hints on. The standard certificate is reachable with hints: "Show me" leads to an echo machine they then crack alone.
  • On track: Where Zero Isn't (good preparation for Episode B).
  • Racing ahead: Faker Finder. Ask: is two inputs ever enough to be sure?
  • Fluent but careless: The X-Ray Specialist. It catches the 2(x + 9) trap.

Certificates show "Assisted: n" honestly. The stretch work printed on each special certificate is the real evidence: read it.

Running the reels

  • Don't announce them. Let the first one be a surprise.
  • About one per student per session; some students get none. That's deliberate.
  • No headphones? Set reels to "Certificate screen only" in teacher mode.
  • House point for anyone who spots a reel's maths joke and explains it.
  • Class Unlock: after the class earns 15 standard certificates across Crack the Fair, Glitch Hunt and The Keeper's Vault, enter POCKET in teacher mode for a premiere on the projector. Two more class words: DOUBLER once the class has played Gus's Forecast, and RAIN after the Final Chain lesson.
