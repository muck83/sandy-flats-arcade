# Under the Pond · Film 0: Application Accepted (draft 5, for review)

How Marcus and Doug end up under the pond. About 220 spoken words, between two and three minutes. No teaching: it ends by
handing the class the four-question opening check. Up top Doug says "Coo." Under the pond Marcus can understand him.

---

## The duck pond

*Sandy Flats, after school. The duck pond. Marcus stands on the bank with a pudding and a spoon beside him. Doug is perched on his helmet.*

**MARCUS:** Look at it, Doug. I saved it. A whole day. Yesterday me would be so proud.

**DOUG:** Coo.

**MARCUS:** One quiz question first. Then pudding. From one, two to four, eight. Across three, up six...

*Doug taps the helmet with his beak. On it, where Marcus can't see it: UP FIRST. THEN ACROSS.*

**DOUG:** Coo. Coo!

**MARCUS:** I don't need the helmet. Three over six. One half!

*Every duck on the pond turns to look at him at the same time. The bank gives way. SPLOSH. The pudding sits on the edge, untouched.*

**MARCUS:** Why is it ALWAYS the duck pond?

*A calm voice, from under the water.*

**VOICE:** The answer was two. Entrance attempt: four. Application accepted.

**MARCUS:** Application? I didn't apply for...

*The pond drains like a bath. Marcus goes down with it. His helmet is left on the mud.*

*Silence. The ducks look at Doug. Doug looks at the pudding. Doug looks at the hole.*

**DOUG:** Coo.

*Doug picks up the helmet in his beak and drops into the hole.*

**TITLE: UNDER THE POND**

## The bottom

*Dark. Marcus lands on something soft. It bleats. The helmet lands on his head. Doug lands on the helmet.*

**VOICE:** Welcome, entrants. One student. One pigeon. The entrance is closed. There are nine floors. The way out is down.

**MARCUS:** Who said that? Where's the pond?

**VOICE:** The pond is the entrance. You have arrived at it four times. The management took this as interest.

**MARCUS:** Those were ACCIDENTS!

**DOUG:** They were wrong answers. I was there for all four.

*Marcus stares at him.*

**MARCUS:** You can TALK?

**DOUG:** I have always been talking. You have not been listening.

**VOICE:** The pigeon is not on the application. The pigeon may leave at any time.

*Marcus looks at Doug. Doug looks up at the circle of daylight far above, then at Marcus.*

**MARCUS:** You jumped in after me.

**DOUG:** You forgot your helmet. It is a safety matter.

## The surface

*The drained pond. Priya and Zero at the edge. The pudding and the spoon.*

**PRIYA:** He left a pudding. Marcus would never leave a pudding.

*Mr. Dunmore arrives. He sees the hole where the pond was, and stops.*

**ZERO:** Mr. Dunmore? Have you seen this before?

**DUNMORE:** *(quietly)* It is a school night. Go home.

*He doesn't move.*

## The check

*The bottom. A door with four lights above it.*

**VOICE:** Before floor one, entrants are assessed. Four questions.

**MARCUS:** Four questions. Easy.

**DOUG:** Complaint one: your confidence.

**VOICE:** You are observed. Observers: one class. The observers will also be assessed. On your own. No talking.

**TITLE: ASSESSMENT BEGINS · Four questions. On your own.**

## Sting

*The pond's edge at dusk. A duck waddles up to the pudding, looks both ways, and puts on a staff badge.*

## Changes from the last draft

- **The tip is on the helmet he is wearing.** Doug taps it and Marcus waves him off, so he is careless, not forgetful. The helmet is left behind on the mud, and Doug carrying it down means he brought the tips.
- **The wrong working is corrected at once:** "The answer was two." It doesn't hang over the diagnostic.
- **The bottom is shorter.** The three-incidents rule has moved to Floor One, where the rusted cart shows it.
- **Doug has no device.** Under the pond Marcus can simply hear him.
- **No "crawlers", achievements, viewers' gifts or excitement meter.** They are entrants being assessed, and the class are observers who are assessed too.
