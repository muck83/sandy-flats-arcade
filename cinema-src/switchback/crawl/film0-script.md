# Under the Pond · Film 0: Application Accepted (draft 6, for review)

How Marcus and Doug end up under the pond. About 260 spoken words, under three minutes. No teaching: it ends by
handing the class the four-question opening check. Up top Doug says "Coo." Under the pond Marcus can understand him.

Draft 6 is about speed. There is a clock on screen through most of the film, the goats are now a threat rather than
just a place to land, and it opens in the middle of the chase.

---

## Cold open: the chase

*Black. Hooves, a lot of them. A tunnel lit red. Marcus sprints toward camera with Doug flapping at his shoulder.
Behind them, a wall of goats with glowing eyes. On screen: DOOR CLOSES 0:09 and GOATS: 40.*

**VOICE:** Entrants. Nine seconds to the door.

**MARCUS:** Doug! Why are the goats ANGRY?

**DOUG:** You landed on them. It is in my report.

*Freeze. 0:07.*

**TITLE: SIX MINUTES EARLIER**

## The duck pond

*Sandy Flats, after school. Marcus on the bank with a pudding. Doug on his helmet.*

**MARCUS:** One quiz question, then pudding. From one, two to four, eight. Across three, up six...

*Doug taps the helmet. On it, where Marcus can't see it: UP FIRST. THEN ACROSS.*

**DOUG:** Coo! Coo!

**MARCUS:** Don't need it. Three over six. One half!

*Every duck turns at once. The bank gives way. SPLOSH.*

**VOICE:** *(from under the water)* The answer was two. Entrance attempt: four. Application accepted. Entrance
closes in thirty seconds.

*On screen: ENTRANCE CLOSES 0:30, ticking. The pond drains like a bath and takes Marcus with it. His helmet is left
on the mud.*

*Doug looks at the pudding. 0:12. Doug looks at the hole. 0:04.*

**DOUG:** Coo.

*He grabs the helmet and dives. 0:01. The hole snaps shut behind his tail feathers.*

**TITLE: UNDER THE POND**

## The pen

*Dark. Marcus lands on something soft. It bleats. Something else bleats. Forty pairs of eyes open, red.*

**VOICE:** Welcome, entrants. Nine floors. The way out is down. You have landed in the penalty pen.

**MARCUS:** The what?

*The helmet lands on his head. Doug lands on the helmet.*

**DOUG:** Penalty: goats. Every wrong answer you have ever given is in this pen.

**MARCUS:** You can TALK?

**DOUG:** I have always been talking. You have not been listening.

*A board lights up: ENTRANTS THIS CYCLE: 12. Names scroll past. One of them flips to REMAINS with a clunk.*

**VOICE:** Entrant eleven has three incidents and remains on floor one. Entrant twelve: you.

*Keith steps out of the herd, wearing a staff badge and a whistle. He blows it.*

**VOICE:** The pen opens in ten seconds. Entrants are advised to run.

**MARCUS:** Run WHERE?

**DOUG:** Away from the goats. That is the whole procedure.

## The chase, again

*Back to the cold open. 0:07. The tunnel, the herd, a door at the end with four unlit lights above it, sliding shut.*

**MARCUS:** We're not going to make it!

**DOUG:** Duck.

**MARCUS:** Where?

**DOUG:** It is an instruction.

*They slide under the door at 0:01. It slams. Forty heads hit the far side: THUD. Silence. One last, small THUD.*

**MARCUS:** *(flat on his back)* Is every floor like this?

**VOICE:** No. Floor one has the Swapper.

*Through the crack under the next door: a huge shadow drags its claws along the wall. Two signs, START and END,
swing round and swap places.*

## The surface

*The drained pond at dusk. Priya and Zero. The pudding, untouched.*

**PRIYA:** He left a pudding. Marcus would never leave a pudding.

*Mr. Dunmore arrives, sees the hole, and stops.*

**ZERO:** Mr. Dunmore? Have you seen this before?

**DUNMORE:** *(quietly)* Go home.

*He doesn't move.*

## The check

*Below. The four lights above the door. Behind them, scratching at the goat door.*

**VOICE:** Before floor one, entrants are assessed. Four questions. Each wrong answer lets one goat through.

**MARCUS:** Four questions. Easy.

**DOUG:** Complaint one: your confidence.

**VOICE:** Observers: one class. You are assessed too. On your own. No talking. Begin.

**TITLE: ASSESSMENT BEGINS · Four questions. On your own.**

## Sting

*The pond at night. A duck waddles up to the pudding, looks both ways, and pins on a staff badge.*

## Changes from draft 5

- **It opens in the chase** and catches up with itself, so the first thing anyone sees is danger.
- **Two clocks on screen:** the thirty seconds before the entrance closes, and the ten seconds before the pen opens.
  The silent looks are kept but timed against the clock, so they build tension instead of filling time.
- **The goats are the threat.** The penalty pen holds every wrong answer, and an angry herd chases them out. The joke
  and the danger finally come from the same thing.
- **Rivals and stakes are shown, not explained:** the entrant board, and entrant eleven flipping to REMAINS.
- **The Swapper is set up** as Floor One's monster.
- **The check has a cost:** each wrong answer lets a goat through. That is in the story only. The real check is
  still done alone and unmarked.
- **The silent beats come down** from about 40 seconds to about 12.
