# Under the Pond · Gate A: The Last Cart Down (401 words, 3:41)

### Gate A

*Clock on screen: GATE A 0:05.*

**MARCUS:** Divide by minus fifty. S is...

**DOUG:** Which way does the sign go?

*Freeze at 0:03.*

**TITLE: TWO MINUTES EARLIER**

### The control room

*A gloved hand chalks the floor number.*

### Gate A

**VOICE:** Gate A. Three locks, one for each floor you passed. Open all three before the last cart leaves.

*Clock on screen: GATE A 0:50.*

**VOICE:** Incidents allowed at the gate: three. Observers, you are assessed after.

**MARCUS:** Who is sitting in our cart?

**VOICE:** That information is on floor nine.

**DOUG:** Complaint six. Somebody is in our cart.

**VOICE:** Lock one. The track runs from post two, height nine, to post six, height one. Dial the slope.

**MARCUS:** Nine take away one, eight. Six take away two, four.

**MARCUS:** Plus two!

**VOICE:** *(quietly)* Stop. Not that one.

*A loose red-eyed goat is watching. The lock buzzes red. BZZZT.*

**VOICE:** Incident. Cause: subtracted in different orders, top and bottom. Penalty: goats.

**VOICE:** *(quietly)* That is how it happened last time.

**MARCUS:** Last time?

**DOUG:** End minus start. Top AND bottom. One take away nine, over six take away two.

**MARCUS:** Minus eight over four. Minus two. It goes DOWNHILL.

*The lock turns green. CLUNK.*

**VOICE:** Correct. Lock one.

**VOICE:** Lock two. The log starts at post two. Write the rule.

**DOUG:** Three more for one post. Six more for two posts. Three a post, every row.

**DOUG:** The start is... There is no row for post zero.

**DOUG:** Coo.

**MARCUS:** Walk it back! Post one, eight. Post zero, five!

**MARCUS:** Height equals three times post, plus five. Check post five. Twenty!

*The lock turns green. CLUNK.*

**VOICE:** Correct. Lock two.

**DOUG:** Complaint seven. You were right and I was not.

*The figure in the last cart turns and looks at them. A gloved hand reaches out of the last cart and snatches seconds off the clock.*

**MARCUS:** He TOOK our seconds!

**VOICE:** Lock three. The counterweight is four fifty. Every sack off takes fifty. The gate lifts under one fifty.

**DOUG:** Those sacks are full of minus signs. The Flippers' sacks.

**MARCUS:** Four fifty take away fifty s, under one fifty. Minus fifty s, under minus three hundred.

**MARCUS:** Divide by minus fifty. S is...

**DOUG:** Which way does the sign go?

**MARCUS:** I KNOW. Turn it over. S MORE than six. Seven sacks!

*Marcus heaves the sacks off the counterweight.*

**DOUG:** Check. Seven off. Four fifty take away three fifty. One hundred.

*The lock turns green. CLUNK. The gate lifts.*

**VOICE:** Correct. Gate A passed.

*The helmet: END MINUS START. / SAME ORDER, TOP AND BOTTOM. / START IS WHERE THE POST IS ZERO. / DIVIDE BY A NEGATIVE: TURN THE SIGN..*

**DOUG:** Three floors. Four lines. You used every one.

*The figure in the last cart turns and looks at them.*

**MARCUS:** Hey! That's OUR cart!

*The last cart rolls away down the chute, with the figure in it.*

**DOUG:** His helmet. It was covered in writing.

**VOICE:** *(quietly)* He has passed this gate before. Many times.

**MARCUS:** Then we catch him on floor four.

## Notes

- **The gate is the Unit 2 check.** One lock per floor: a slope from two points (Floor 1), a rule from a log that does not start at post zero (Floor 2), and an inequality where the sign turns (Floor 3).
- **Marcus's mistake** is the one that kept the Voice on Floor One: the right numbers, subtracted in different orders top and bottom. The Voice says "Stop" before it files, and lets slip that this is how it happened last time.
- **Doug stops dead** at the second lock: there is no row for post zero, so there is no procedure, so "Coo." Marcus walks it back. This is the film's moment where Marcus is right and Doug isn't.
- **Marcus turns the sign himself** at the third lock. The sacks on the counterweight are the Flippers' stolen minus signs.
- **The Foreman in person, at a distance:** a silhouette in a helmet covered in writing, sitting in the last cart. He snatches seconds off the clock and rides away when the gate opens. The Voice: he has passed this gate before, many times.
- **It ends by handing the class the Gate A check,** done alone and in silence, as Film 0 handed them the opening check. The lesson (L24) holds the check and the Boss Raid.
